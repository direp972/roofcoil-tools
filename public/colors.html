Metal roof colors

72 colors across 4 paint lines and the bare metals — with the 24 you can match from more than one mill, and the shops that stock each one

Color is usually the last thing decided and the first thing that holds a job up, because the shop that rolls your profile may not stock your color. Every color below links to the listed shops that carry it, so you can check before you promise a homeowner.

Screens lie. Gloss, gauge, sun angle and the finish system all change how a color reads on a finished roof. Use these as a shortlist, then confirm against a physical chip from the shop that will roll the panel. The Color Lab renders any of them on an actual seam profile, which gets you closer than a flat square.

Quick answers

How many metal roof colors does RoofCoil list?

72 distinct colors across 4 paint lines plus bare copper, Galvalume, G90 galvanized and bonderized paint-grip steel. Each one links to the shops in the directory that stock it.

Can I match a color across manufacturers?

Sometimes. 24 of the 68 painted colors are offered under the same name by more than one manufacturer, so a repair or an addition does not have to go back to the original mill. The other 44 are specific to one line. Names matching is not the same as the paint matching — always confirm against a physical chip from the shop that will roll the panel.

Is the color on screen accurate?

No. Gloss level, gauge, the sun angle and the finish system all change how a color reads on a finished roof, and a screen cannot show any of that. Treat these swatches as a shortlist, then confirm against a physical chip before you commit a job.

What does premium mean on a color?

29 of these are premium finishes — usually the metallics and the deep bases, which cost more per square because of the pigment. The panel calculator prices them separately.

Which colors need PVDF rather than SMP?

Reds, bright blues and the dark bronzes fade and chalk fastest in SMP, so they are the ones most worth specifying in PVDF (Kynar 500). Both systems carry 40-year paint warranties from most mills, but the warranty measures chalk and fade numbers rather than promising the roof still matches new material.
