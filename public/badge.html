Why we are asking

Your listing page shows everything your shop runs — every seam profile, every screw-down shape, the gutter profiles, your colors and each of your plants. A contractor who finds it can see what you can make before they call, which means fewer calls about work you do not do.

The badge works both ways. It gives the contractors already on your website a way to see that lineup, and it helps anyone searching for what you roll find you here in the first place. It is free, it will stay free, and there is nothing to sign.
